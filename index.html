Eat with intention

Know what's really on your plate

NutriTrack helps students and busy people understand their daily energy needs,log meals in seconds ,and build habits that actually stick -no spreadsheets requied.

Why it matters

Most diet advice is generic guesswork

Your calorie needs depend on your age,weight,height and how active you really are-not a one-size-fits-all chart. NutriTrack calculates a number that's acutually yours,then helps you track how close you get to it every day.

Personal calculator

Enter your stats once and get a daily calorie target build around your activity level, not an average strainger's.

Simple food log.

Add what you eat, attach a photo if you like and watch your your day build up in a clean,readable table.

Build on Real Science

Calculations use the Mifflin-St Jeor equations,the same formula dietitions rely on, explained in plain language.
